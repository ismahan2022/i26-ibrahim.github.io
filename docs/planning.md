# Project Proposal

## Business Case

The business case for a car park is to help feel more comfortable by using our app to search for parking information such as location, pricing, availability, and operating hours

### Problem statement
TODO: Replace the example text like this with text that describes your project. What is the problem to be addressed?

Trying to find parking in Bristol is very difficult as there isn't enough reliable information available online about operating times, pricing and space availability. This causes wasted time when trying to travel anywhere in Bristol. To address this issue, our project will use data from Open Bristol to make an app where you can see key details such as prices, operating hours, space availability and other useful updates.
### Business benefits
TODO: List high-level benefits that this product will provide.

The benefits are that drivers can save time, avoid stress, and plan ahead by accessig real-time parking information, such as operating hours, pricing, and space availability all in one easy-to-use app.
