# Project Proposal

## Business Case

The business case for a car park is to help feel more comfortable by using our app to search for parking information such as location, pricing, availability, and operating hours.

### Problem statement

Trying to find parking in Bristol is very difficult as there isn't enough reliable information available online about operating times, pricing and space availability. 

This causes wasted time when trying to travel anywhere in Bristol. 

To address this issue, our project will use data from Open Bristol to make an app where you can see key details such as prices, operating hours, space availability and other useful updates.

### Business benefits

The benfits are the app will make parking in Bristol quicker and less stressful by bringing together key information such as location, pricing and availability in one place. 
By allowing users to share real-time updates, this will improve the accuracy and usefulness of the data.
This will help drivers plan their journeys more efficiently and reduce time spent searching for spaces and cut down on congestion and emissions.

### Options Considered

Google Maps provides parking locations near the user and allows them to leave reviews about their experiences. It is one of the most popular and easily accessible tools for navigation and is available on almost all moblie devices. 
Its main purpose is to provide directions rather than detailed parking information and while it can help users find the nearest car park and navigate there easily but it does not include important details such as the number of spaces available, current pricing or real-time availability. 
At times the data can be inconsistent or incorrect and updates about closures, maintenance work or new car parks can take a while to appear and this makes it less reliable for users who need accurate and up-to-date parking information.

Parkopedia is a dedicated parking information platform that provides detailed data on car parks including locations, opening hours and in some cases pricing and availability. 
It is more focused on parking than Google Maps and includes coverage across the UK. 
However while it offers more detail it still does not provide truly real-time updates for all car parks and some information may depend on user submissions or outdated records. 
The app includes paid features for advanced functionality which may limit accessibility for some users.


### Expected Risks
There are a few potential risks associated with this project. 

The main challenge is that the data from the Bristol Open Data portal is not updated in real time meaning information about car park availability, closures or pricing may sometimes be out of date.

Although the app will allow users to submit live updates this approach relies on active participation and inaccurate or misleading information can cause problems. 

Technical issues such as delays in updating data or errors when combining user input with official data may also occur. 
Additionally, external factors such as unexpected car park closures, maintenance work, weather conditions or changes in pricing could affect the accuracy of the information displayed. 

Ensuring user trust and maintaining data reliability will therefore be key challenges.
